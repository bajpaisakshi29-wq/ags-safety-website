About Us

Built for the Work That Matters.

Safety footwear should do more than protect — it should perform, endure and inspire confidence with every step. For decades, we've combined craftsmanship, engineering and manufacturing discipline to build footwear for the world's most demanding workplaces, from industrial floors to defence and high-risk environments — all designed, built and tested under one roof.

Today we serve 25+ countries across Europe, the Middle East, Latin America and beyond, across PU/PU, PU-Rubber, Direct Vulcanized Rubber and Cemented constructions. Manufacturing is only part of the story — we're a family of engineers and craftsmen who take pride in giving someone the confidence to take their next step safely.

Certifications Held
Last development, material trials, and sample rounds all run on-site before a design ever reaches the floor — so fit and finish are settled long before volume production starts.
Cutting, stitching, PU direct-injection molding, and assembly run across dedicated lines, built to hold consistent spec whether the order is 500 pairs or 50,000.
Slip resistance, toe impact, electrical hazard, and chemical resistance are all verified in-house against CE, ISO, and BIS benchmarks before a single batch clears for export.
Every batch carries QC checkpoints and full documentation, so certifications and origin can be traced back to the line and the day it was made.

Responsible on the Floor

Every pair here is made under the same roof shown above — never subcontracted. That means the people stitching, testing and packing your order are people we work with directly, on equipment we maintain ourselves, under the ISI, ISO and CE processes already covering this range. It also means less waste: footwear that fails in-house testing gets caught before it ever reaches a shipment, not after.
